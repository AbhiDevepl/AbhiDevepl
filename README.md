I'm a Computer Engineering student at Universal College of Engineering & Research, Pune, and the founder of **[ExDevX](https://exdevx.in)**, a web/app development agency I started in 2022. I design and ship production systems for real clients — not portfolio filler — while finishing my degree.

- 🤖 Building AI-integrated products: mock interview systems, automation tooling, agentic workflows
- 🖥️ Run self-hosted infrastructure (Dokploy, Traefik, VPS) for client and personal deployments
- 🎯 Looking for internships in **full-stack, backend, or AI integration** tracks
